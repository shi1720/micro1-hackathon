City Hall

Council approves long-awaited Maple Street bridge repair

By Dana Whitfield · Staff Writer · Updated 7:42 a.m.

Crews staged equipment at the north approach Friday evening, hours after the vote.

After eleven years of studies, three failed bond measures, and one memorable town hall that ran past midnight, the city council voted 6–1 on Friday to fund a full rehabilitation of the Maple Street bridge.

The $4.2 million project will replace the deck, reinforce both piers, and add a protected lane for bikes and strollers — a concession to the parents' coalition that packed every public comment session since March. Work begins the Tuesday after Labor Day and is expected to run through May.

“Nobody gets everything they wanted,” said Councilmember Ruth Okonkwo, who cast the lone dissenting vote over the detour plan. “But the bridge my grandmother drove a milk truck across will still be here for my grandkids. That counts for something.”

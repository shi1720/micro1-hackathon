Services

One clinic for the whole household. If you need care we do not provide in-house, we will make the referral and chase the paperwork so you do not have to.

Well-child visits

Growth checks, school and sports physicals, and honest answers to the questions you thought of at 2 a.m.

Chronic care

Steady, unhurried management of diabetes, blood pressure, asthma, and thyroid conditions.

Same-day sick care

Morning slots held back every weekday for fevers, earaches, rashes, and things that cannot wait.

Immunizations

Childhood series, annual flu shots, and travel vaccines, with records sent straight to schools on request.

Women's health

Annual exams, contraception counseling, and menopause care in a practice that listens first.

Minor procedures

Stitches, mole removal, joint injections, and wart treatment, done here so you skip the hospital bill.

New here? It takes three steps.

Call the front desk, pick a time that suits your week, and come meet us. Bring your insurance card and medication list to the first visit; we handle transferring records from your previous practice.

Most new-patient visits are scheduled within two weeks, and we reserve extra time for the first appointment so nobody feels processed.

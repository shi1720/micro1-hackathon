Moonrise Yoga

Slow down. Breathe deep. Begin again.

A neighborhood studio in Willow Creek offering small, unhurried classes for every body and every level of experience. No mirrors, no pressure — just warm light, quiet music, and room to breathe.

Our classes

Vinyasa Flow

A steady, breath-led sequence that builds heat and balance. Expect standing flows, gentle inversions, and a long, well-earned rest. All levels welcome.

Candlelight Restorative

Our slowest evening class. Supported postures held for several minutes, guided breathing, and a short seated meditation under soft candlelight.

Beginner Foundations

A four-week introduction to the essential postures, taught slowly and with plenty of props. Perfect if you have never stepped on a mat before.

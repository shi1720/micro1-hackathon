Our Story

Wildflour began in a rented church kitchen with one secondhand deck oven and a sourdough starter named Clementine. Ten years on, Clementine is still rising every night, and we are still folding, shaping, and scoring every loaf by hand.

We mill a portion of our flour in-house from grain grown in the Willamette Valley, and everything on the counter was baked the same morning you buy it. No freezers, no shortcuts, no preservatives — just flour, water, salt, and patience.

When the case is empty, we close. Most Saturdays that happens well before lunch, so set an alarm.

Note: [EcomBack's annual report](https://www.ecomback.com/annual-2025-ada-website-accessibility-lawsuit-report) uses a narrower methodology and counts 3,948 for 2025 (vs. 3,188 in 2024, 3,862 in 2023 — a 23.84% YoY increase). Both trackers agree on direction: **2025 was a record year**.

### Trend and structure (UsableNet 2025 PDF unless noted)

- 1,427 lawsuits in 2025 targeted companies **already sued before**; repeat defendants were 45% of federal cases (41% in 2024).
- E-commerce = **70% of all 2025 suits** (77% in 2024); food service 21%; healthcare 2%.
- 36% of the top 500 e-commerce retailers were sued in 2025 alone; 82% of the top 500 have been sued since 2018 (2024 PDF).
- Plaintiff concentration: top 10 plaintiff firms filed 77% of all cases (2024 PDF). EcomBack: 16 firms filed 90.35% of 2025 suits; a single plaintiff filed 241.

### Share targeting small/medium businesses

- **64% of 2025 defendants had annual revenue under $25M** (36% over $25M, up from 33% in 2024 and 27% in 2023) — [UsableNet 2025 PDF](https://info.usablenet.com/hubfs/Remediated%20-%202025_Year-End_Digital_Accessibility_Lawsuit_Report_FINAL.pdf).
- Historical: <$25M share was 67% (2024), 73% (2023), 72% (2022), 70% (2021, 2020) — [UsableNet 2024 PDF](https://3280432.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/3280432/UsableNet-2024-Year-End-ADA-Web-Lawsuit-Report.pdf).

### Settlement / legal cost ranges

- Typical settlements **$5,000–$20,000**; small businesses often settle for under $5,000–$10,000 — [Accessible.org](https://accessible.org/ada-website-compliance-lawsuit-settlement-amounts/).
- Defense legal fees commonly estimated at **$10,000–$50,000+** per suit (initial response $2k–$5k, discovery $5k–$15k, trial prep $15k–$50k+) — [TestParty](https://testparty.ai/blog/ada-lawsuit-cost-statistics-settlement-defense-data), [ADA Scanner](https://adascanner.org/blog/ada-lawsuit-cost). **Caveat:** vendor-published estimates, not court data.

## 2. European Accessibility Act (EAA)

- **Enforcement date: 28 June 2025** — Directive (EU) 2019/882, [EUR-Lex official text](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj/eng); [European Commission official EAA page](https://commission.europa.eu/strategy-and-policy/policies/justice-and-fundamental-rights/disability/union-equality-strategy-rights-persons-disabilities-2021-2030/european-accessibility-act_en).
- **Who must comply** (EC official page): e-commerce, banking services, e-books, telephony, audiovisual media services, transport ticketing/check-in, ATMs, computers/OS, smartphones. Applies to **any company selling in-scope products/services to EU consumers, including non-EU companies** — [Deque EAA FAQ](https://www.deque.com/blog/european-accessibility-act-eaa-top-20-key-questions-answered/).
- **Microenterprise exemption**: service providers with <10 employees AND ≤€2M turnover exempt from services requirements (Art. 4(5)) — [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2019/882/oj/eng).
- **Penalties (set nationally — examples traced to national law):**
  - **Ireland**: fines up to **€60,000 and/or up to 18 months imprisonment**; officers/directors personally liable — [Mason Hayes & Curran](https://www.mhc.ie/latest/insights/european-accessibility-act-implemented-into-irish-law).
  - **Germany**: up to **€100,000** — [Level Access](https://www.levelaccess.com/blog/penalties-for-eaa-non-compliance/).
  - **France**: **€50,000** per violation + €25,000 for missing accessibility statements — [Level Access](https://www.levelaccess.com/blog/penalties-for-eaa-non-compliance/).
  - **Italy**: up to **5% of annual turnover** — [Level Access](https://www.levelaccess.com/blog/penalties-for-eaa-non-compliance/).
- **Early enforcement**: within days of the deadline, French disability organizations issued formal legal notices to four major grocery retailers — [Pivotal Accessibility](https://www.pivotalaccessibility.com/2025/09/eaa-enforcement-in-europe-following-the-june-2025-deadline/).
- **How many businesses affected: no verified official figure exists** — we deliberately do not cite one. The commonly cited **~87 million people in the EU with a disability** figure comes from the EU's Disability Rights Strategy 2021–2030.

## 3. Cost of manual audits & remediation

- Manual WCAG audits: **most clients pay $1,250–$2,750** — [Accessible.org pricing page](https://accessible.org/pricing/) (primary vendor pricing).
- Manual audits **$1,500–$5,000** by complexity — [Skynet Technologies](https://www.skynettechnologies.com/blog/ada-wcag-website-accessibility-compliance-manual-audit-cost); professional audits for mid-size sites **$5,000–$25,000** — [TestParty](https://testparty.ai/blog/accessibility-audit-cost).
- Per-page audit pricing: **$100–$300/page** — [ProjectCostEstimator](https://projectcostestimator.com/blog/wcag-accessibility-cost-2026).
- Remediation: **$350–$550/page**; a 10-page site ≈ $3,500 audit + $5,500 remediation — [ProjectCostEstimator](https://projectcostestimator.com/blog/wcag-accessibility-cost-2026).
- Full audit + remediation of a medium-complexity web app to WCAG 2.2 AA: **$25,000–$75,000 all-in** — [Kanopy Labs](https://kanopylabs.com/blog/how-much-does-accessibility-audit-wcag-remediation-cost).
- **Caveat:** all practitioner-published figures; they cluster consistently, which is the best available evidence.

## 4. What % of WCAG issues automated scanners detect

- **Deque's 57% claim (verified)**: Deque's study of 2,000+ audits / ~13,000 pages / ~300,000 issues found axe automation covered **57% of accessibility issues by volume** — [Deque blog](https://www.deque.com/blog/automated-testing-study-identifies-57-percent-of-digital-accessibility-issues/), [full report](https://www.deque.com/automated-accessibility-coverage-report/).
- **The older ~30–40% figures (verified)**: UK Government Digital Service built a test page with **143 documented barriers**; the best automated tool detected only **~40%** and tools ranged roughly 17–41% — [GDS Accessibility Blog](https://accessibility.blog.gov.uk/2017/02/24/what-we-found-when-we-tested-tools-on-the-worlds-least-accessible-webpage/) ([test suite](https://github.com/alphagov/accessibility-tool-audit)).
- Practitioner rule of thumb: only **~25–33% of WCAG success criteria** are reliably automatable — [The A11Y Project](https://www.a11yproject.com/posts/automated-tools-can-ensure-full-accessibility-compliance/).
- **Honest framing StepFree adopts**: we claim "automated fix of the scanner-detectable layer, verified by re-scan, plus human-routed drafts for the judgment layer" — never "full WCAG compliance" (that claim is what the FTC fined accessiBe for, §6).

## 5. WebAIM Million — latest stats

**2026 report (published Feb 2026)** — [webaim.org/projects/million](https://webaim.org/projects/million/):

- **95.9%** of the top 1M home pages had detectable WCAG 2 A/AA failures (up from 94.8% — the web got *worse*).
- Average **56.1 errors per home page** (+10.1% vs 2025).
- Top error types (% of home pages): **low-contrast text 83.9%; missing alt text 53.1%; missing form labels 51%; empty links 46.3%; empty buttons 30.6%; missing document language 13.5%**.
- **133M+ ARIA attributes detected (~133/page, +27% YoY)**; pages *with* ARIA averaged 59.1 errors vs. 42 without.

**2025 report** — [webaim.org/projects/million/2025](https://webaim.org/projects/million/2025): 94.8% failure rate; **51 errors/page**; six error types = 96% of all errors (low contrast 79.1%, missing alt 55.5%, form labels 48.2%, empty links 45.4%, empty buttons 29.6%, missing lang 15.8%).

**Caveat:** WebAIM counts only *automatically detectable* errors — true failure rates are higher. Our fixture suite mirrors this distribution (see `fixtures/README.md`).

## 6. Overlay widgets controversy

### FTC action against accessiBe — verified on FTC.gov

- **Jan 3, 2025**: FTC ordered accessiBe to pay **$1,000,000** over deceptive claims that its overlay widget could make any website WCAG 2.1 AA compliant — [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/01/ftc-order-requires-online-marketer-pay-1-million-deceptive-claims-its-ai-product-could-make-websites).
- **April 2025**: final order approved (3-0) — [FTC press release](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2025/04/ftc-approves-final-order-requiring-accessibe-pay-1-million); [FTC docket 2223156](https://www.ftc.gov/legal-library/browse/cases-proceedings/2223156-accessibe-inc).

### Sites get sued *while using* overlays (primary tracker data)

- **2024: 1,023 companies with a live accessibility widget were sued** (~25% of all suits) — [UsableNet 2024 PDF](https://3280432.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/3280432/UsableNet-2024-Year-End-ADA-Web-Lawsuit-Report.pdf).
- **2025: ~1,416 widget-defendant filings** (sum of monthly chart; peak 155 in July). The UsableNet 2025 PDF states outright: "Accessibility Widgets Do Not Reduce Legal Risk… Widgets typically do not resolve underlying code-level accessibility issues."
- EcomBack: **983 lawsuits (24.9% of 2025 filings)** hit widget-equipped sites; **accessiBe was the widget on 424 (43.1%)** — [EcomBack](https://www.ecomback.com/annual-2025-ada-website-accessibility-lawsuit-report).

### Overlay Fact Sheet

- **1,031 signatories** (as of Aug 2026): no overlay "can cause a website to become fully compliant with any existing accessibility standard" — [overlayfactsheet.com](https://overlayfactsheet.com/en/).

### Revenue / valuation (shows the money in this market)

### Differentiation verdict

The dominant revenue in this market is **overlay/runtime JS** — which the FTC fined, ~25% of lawsuits ignore, and 1,031 experts have publicly repudiated. Almost nobody ships an autonomous loop of *scan → fix actual source → re-verify → prove no damage*. The caveats we build into our claims: (1) the loop can only verify the scanner-detectable layer (~57% by volume, Deque's own number) — so StepFree routes the judgment layer to humans instead of claiming it; (2) incumbents (Deque MCP) are moving into this space, which confirms the thesis rather than undermining it.
